## Prompts finais

**Colorida:** “Create one polished, original, horizontal logo for the fictional photography and wedding film studio ENTREVER. A compact distinctive symbol on the left and the exact word ‘ENTREVER’ on the right; below it, ‘FOTOGRAFIA E FILMES’. Express seeing or revealing something through subtle framing and negative space, with a monogram-like character. Quiet editorial sophistication, precise geometric construction, custom-looking clean sans serif, uppercase, deliberate spacing. Flat warm near-black `#261D1D`, with muted aged copper `#A66B4F` in one small intentional portion. Transparent background. No gradients, shadows, 3D, textures, mockups, decorations, extra words, or watermark.”

**Escura:** “Preserve the colored base logo’s geometry, typography, layout, wording, and proportions. Recolor only the copper accent to solid warm near-black `#261D1D`. Transparent background. No gradients or other effects.”

**Clara:** “Faithfully match the base logo’s symbol, typography, wording, and horizontal layout. Recolor every part of the logo to solid warm ivory `#F2E8DF`, including the triangle. Present it on a dark brown `#261D1D` background. No gradients, shadows, or additional elements.”

Os prompts foram refinados durante a geração para tentar manter o desenho e controlar a transparência. A versão clara ficou mais limpa como imagem de apresentação sobre fundo escuro.
