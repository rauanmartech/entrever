# Imagens dos cards de fotografia

Três fotografias quadradas, 1254 × 1254 px, geradas com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`. Devem ser exibidas com recorte quadrado e bordas arredondadas pelo layout do site. Nenhuma imagem contém texto ou elementos de interface.

- `formatura.png`: capelo preto em destaque, pessoa em segundo plano, luz quente.
- `casamento.png`: casal recém-casado em momento íntimo, véu e traje escuro.
- `newborn.png`: recém-nascido nos braços de um dos pais, tons claros.

## Prompts finais

**Formatura:** “Square editorial photograph for a small service card: close-up of a matte black graduation cap with tassel worn by a young adult graduate, cap unmistakable at thumbnail size. Soft cream architectural background out of focus, natural window light, warm muted editorial grading. Central safe area for rounded-square crop. No text, logo, border or overlay.”

**Casamento:** “Square editorial photograph for a small service card: intimate candid close portrait of newlyweds, bride in simple ivory dress and veil, groom in understated dark suit, foreheads close and hands lightly touching. Warm cream and taupe blurred background, natural light, restrained earthy grading. Both faces and veil in central safe area. No text, logo, border or overlay.”

**Newborn:** “Square editorial photograph for a small service card: real newborn resting safely in a parent's supportive arms, face visible and eyes gently closed, soft ivory cotton, one small hand visible. Warm pale cream and taupe setting, natural window light, realistic proportions, central safe area for rounded-square crop. No text, logo, border, costume or overlay.”
