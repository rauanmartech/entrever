# Fotógrafo da Entrever — proposta v2

Arquivos gerados com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, em PNG com transparência. Esta versão substitui a primeira proposta para avaliação; os arquivos anteriores foram mantidos como histórico.

## Direção do personagem

Homem branco de aparência cotidiana e criativa, olhos castanhos, rosto sem barba, cabelo platinado penteado para trás. Roupa indie discreta: camiseta preta, jaqueta de trabalho grafite, calça escura e botas usadas. Câmera profissional em gesto natural. Sem tatuagens.

## Prompts finais

**Corpo inteiro:** “Create a new original fictional white male photographer in his early-to-mid 30s, average build and natural, unpolished appearance, not a fashion model. Brown eyes, clean-shaven face, natural skin texture, slightly asymmetrical everyday features. Platinum-blond hair intentionally combed straight back. Faded black crew-neck T-shirt, relaxed charcoal workwear jacket, straight black trousers and practical worn boots. Camera held casually at his side. Full body, candid pose, realistic editorial photography, soft light, genuinely transparent background. No tattoos, hat, vest, branding or scenery.”

**Meio corpo:** “Use the full-body image as the exact identity reference. Show the same photographer from waist upward with brown eyes, clean-shaven face, platinum hair combed back, black T-shirt and charcoal workwear jacket. Camera naturally in one hand, candid three-quarter posture, realistic editorial light, precise transparent cutout. No tattoos or change of identity.”
